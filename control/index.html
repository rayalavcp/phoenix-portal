Connect your Portal

Phoenix Control talks to the device over USB, right from this page. Nothing to install.

  1. Turn on ADB. On the Portal: Settings → Debugging → ADB enabled (Portal TV: Settings → System → Developer options).
  2. Plug it in with a USB-C cable that carries data.
  3. Click Connect, pick the Portal, then tap Allow on its screen.

This browser can't use USB. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
