# Kids Portal maps

Packed by the Kids Portal's map tool from public map data: each region's
outline simplified and scaled into a box 1000 units wide.

- `usa.json`: U.S. state boundaries from the U.S. Census Bureau (public
  domain), via [us-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas)
  (ISC licence), Albers projection with Alaska and Hawaii inset.
- `india.json`: [India boundaries](https://github.com/datameet/maps) by the
  [DataMeet India community](http://datameet.org/),
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed:
  simplified; Ladakh made a union territory (the former Leh and Kargil
  districts and the area north of about 35.1°N of the unadministered
  area); Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu merged; names spelled
  as today.
- `world.json`: [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) admin-0
  countries, India's point of view (public domain), Robinson projection.
